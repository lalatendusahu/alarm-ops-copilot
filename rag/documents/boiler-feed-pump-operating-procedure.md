# Boiler Feed Pump Operating Procedure (Unit 1)

## Scope

Covers Boiler Feed Pump 101 and Boiler Feed Pump 102, both centrifugal multistage pumps
supplying feedwater to the Unit 1 steam drum. One pump normally runs while the other is on
standby, with automatic changeover on low discharge pressure.

## Normal Operating Limits

- Discharge pressure: 140-160 bar
- Bearing vibration (velocity RMS): below 4.5 mm/s normal, 4.5-7.1 mm/s alert, above 7.1 mm/s
  requires shutdown evaluation
- Bearing temperature: below 85 C
- Seal leakage: visually dry to a light weep; continuous dripping is not acceptable

## Response to High Bearing Vibration Alarm

1. Confirm the reading against the local vibration monitor; do not rely solely on the alarm.
2. Check for a recent change in flow rate, suction pressure, or standby-pump changeover that
   could explain a transient spike.
3. If vibration remains elevated for more than 15 minutes, notify the shift supervisor and
   review recent maintenance history for the pump -- a recurring high bearing vibration alarm
   on the same pump within a 90-day window is a strong indicator of bearing wear or misalignment
   rather than a transient process upset, and should not be repeatedly acknowledged without
   escalation.
4. Do not attempt to increase flow to "ride through" a vibration alarm. If vibration exceeds
   7.1 mm/s, initiate changeover to the standby pump and remove the affected pump from service
   for inspection.
5. Log the event and reference any prior bearing replacement or vibration analysis work orders
   for the same asset.

## Response to Seal Leak Detected

Isolate and monitor; a light weep is acceptable but any visible dripping or spray requires the
pump to be scheduled for seal replacement at the next available outage. Do not run a pump with
active seal leakage past the current shift without supervisor sign-off.

## Startup Sequence Notes

Vibration readings are expected to be transiently elevated for the first 2-3 minutes after
startup as the pump reaches thermal equilibrium; this is not itself an indication of a fault
unless it persists beyond 5 minutes or exceeds the alert threshold.
