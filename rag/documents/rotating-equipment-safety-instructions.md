# Rotating Equipment Safety Instructions

## Lockout / Tagout

Before any physical inspection, bearing replacement, or coupling work on a pump, compressor,
turbine, or motor, the asset must be fully isolated and locked out per site procedure. Verify
zero energy state (electrical, stored rotational, and hydraulic) before removing any guard.

## Personal Protective Equipment

Hearing protection is required in all rotating equipment areas above 85 dB. Safety glasses and
steel-toed boots are required at all times. Additional hand and face protection is required when
working near an open coupling or exposed shaft.

## Vibration Monitoring Near Running Equipment

Handheld vibration readings should be taken from designated measurement points only. Do not
place hands or tools near a coupling or shaft while the machine is running.

## Escalation for Unsafe Conditions

Any condition that appears to create an immediate risk to personnel -- exposed rotating parts,
missing guards, active leaks near ignition sources -- must be reported to the shift supervisor
immediately and the equipment tagged out of service, regardless of production impact.
